How many months until I speak Irish?

The question

🎯❓

Speak how? Greetings? Chat? A TEG level?

The catch

⏱📝

Forget months. I'll count my study hours.

The fix

💡💪

Hour 60: I can chat about the weather!

Payoff

  • Define "learn" first. Greetings, everyday conversation, a TEG level and comfortable fluency are different timelines. Pick the goal before you pick a deadline.
  • Rough numbers. About 50-100 hours for basic phrases, 300-600 for simple conversation, and 1,000-2,000 for comfortable fluency. Treat these as rough guides, not promises.
  • Irish has extra hurdles. Mutations, VSO word order and conjugated prepositions take extra time. Counting hours keeps you honest.
