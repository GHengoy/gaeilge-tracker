Payoff

  • Rough targets. About 100-200 words for survival phrases, 500-1,000 for basic conversation, 1,500-2,500 for comfortable chat. Learn the useful words first.
  • Frequency beats size. The most common few hundred words cover most of what you hear and say. Test yourself by saying them aloud.
  • Active is smaller than passive. Words you can produce on demand are a harder-won set than words you merely recognise. Raise the target once it feels easy.
