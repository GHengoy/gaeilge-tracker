Online meetup

"Tá mé ag déanamh tae." Recording one minute daily.

The fix

👩🙂

"Dia is Muire duit! Is mise Áine."

Five weeks later

  • Narrate your day aloud. Describe what you are doing in simple Irish while you cook, walk or tidy up.
  • Record one minute and listen back. Compare it with native audio from RnaG or TG4 and shadow the lines you stumble on.
  • Count speaking minutes separately. Solo talk is real practice, so give it its own number instead of lumping it in.

The Irish Gaelic Self-Study Progress Tracker at https://kimnet8.gumroad.com/l/kanmzp has a speaking column for solo practice and a notes column for the phrases that tangle your tongue. A week of five-minute entries shows the silence is finally broken.
