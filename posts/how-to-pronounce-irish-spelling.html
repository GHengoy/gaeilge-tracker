Six weeks later

  • Learn broad and slender first. Consonants next to a, o, u sound different from those next to e, i, and the spelling marks which is which.
  • One sound pattern a week. Take bh/mh, then dh/gh, then the urú pairs like "bp", instead of memorising a whole chart.
  • Check with native audio. Teanglann.ie plays words in Munster, Connacht and Ulster voices, so you hear the real sound, not a guess.
