Day 30

  • Use the core columns. Date, hours, vocabulary, grammar, minutes per skill, streak and notes. Fill in what you know and skip the rest.
  • Log honestly. Real numbers, including your 15-minute days, reveal real patterns. Weekly backfilling is fine. A messy honest log beats a perfect empty one.
  • Review monthly. Check time per skill, vocabulary growth and streaks, then plan the next month. Then adjust next month's plan to match.
