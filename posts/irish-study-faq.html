Payoff

  • Pick one dialect. Connacht is a sensible default; branch out around B1-B2. Switching too early can feel confusing.
  • Grammar is different, not impossible. VSO word order, mutations and prepositional pronouns become familiar with practice. These are normal hurdles, so keep going.
  • Find speaking partners. Language exchange apps, online tutors, solo recordings and learner communities all help. A little contact each week helps.
