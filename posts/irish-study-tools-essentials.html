Payoff

  • Start with core references. A print dictionary, a self-paced grammar textbook and a phrasebook cover the basics. They cover lookup, structure and real conversation.
  • Make your own cards. Handwriting flashcards and keeping a vocabulary journal deepen memory. Both approaches help, so mix them.
  • Read children's books. Bilingual readers give context and quick self-checks. Short checks keep you moving.
