Six weeks later

  • A streak measures habit, not skill. Duolingo is great for daily recall, but it mostly trains reading and tapping answers.
  • Speaking and listening need their own time. Add native audio like RnaG or TG4 and a few minutes of speaking aloud each day.
  • Log minutes by skill. A weekly split shows which skill the app is quietly skipping.

The Irish Gaelic Self-Study Progress Tracker at https://kimnet8.gumroad.com/l/kanmzp logs listening, speaking, reading and writing time in separate columns, so a long app streak can't hide a zero in speaking. Keep the streak, and let the weekly totals tell you what to add next.
