Payoff

  • It's more than language. Heritage learning rebuilds a bridge to family history, culture and identity. Learning here is personal, not only academic.
  • Feelings are valid. Grief, shame and imposter syndrome are common, and many learners move through them. You are not alone in feeling this.
  • Bursts are fine. Episodic study still adds up, as long as you keep a record. You belong here. Age and level do not matter.
