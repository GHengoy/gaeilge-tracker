Payoff

  • Don't start from zero. Listening and reading usually hold up; speaking and writing fade faster. Skills fade at different speeds.
  • Check, don't guess. A fifteen-minute skills check shows which skill needs the most attention. Look at the result, then plan.
  • Habit first. Ten to fifteen minutes a day for a week, then build up over two or three weeks. Small restarts feel doable.
