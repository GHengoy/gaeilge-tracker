Week 11

  • Know the exam. TEG has six CEFR-aligned levels (A1-C2) and tests each skill separately. It runs twice a year.
  • Log evidence. Record target level, section, mock scores, days to exam and target percentage. Numbers beat hopeful feelings.
  • Read the trend. Rising scores mean keep going, a plateau means change something, a decline is an alarm bell. Plan backward from the exam date.
