A month on

  • The gap is normal. Reading outpaces listening because text gives you time and word boundaries. Speech blends and shortens words without warning.
  • Find the cause. Speed, dialect and vocabulary problems each need a different fix. Then drill the right problem.
  • Climb a ladder. Graded audio, slowed native audio, short native clips, then longer unscripted content. Move up only when a step feels comfortable.
